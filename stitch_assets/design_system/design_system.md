## Brand & Style
The design system is built to bridge the gap between traditional Indian commerce and digital efficiency. It targets residents and shop owners in Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns, prioritizing familiarity over "flashy" tech aesthetics.

The style is **Warm Minimalism**. It utilizes a soft, organic base to create an inviting atmosphere that feels like a trusted neighborhood storefront. By avoiding complex gradients, aggressive blurs, or high-velocity animations, the interface remains calm and dependable. The goal is to evoke a sense of "digital civic pride"—a platform that belongs to the community it serves.
